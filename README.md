dotsy is an agentic platform that enables users to build, edit, and publish their online presence using AI assistants like Claude, ChatGPT, or Cursor. It offers a free, no‑card service that integrates AI-driven website creation, content management, and publishing, positioning itself as a modern solution for personal and business web presence.

**Areas:** Company · AI · Website Builder · No-Code · Free

### APIs (16)

- [dotsy Account API](https://apis.io/apis/dotsy-ai/dotsy-ai-account-api/) — Your account, its workspaces and its API keys.
- [dotsy Assets API](https://apis.io/apis/dotsy-ai/dotsy-ai-assets-api/) — Upload, list and delete images, fonts and other files a site serves.
- [dotsy Brief API](https://apis.io/apis/dotsy-ai/dotsy-ai-brief-api/) — The site brief: what the site is for, who it serves and how it should sound.
- [dotsy Collections API](https://apis.io/apis/dotsy-ai/dotsy-ai-collections-api/) — Define the fields of a site's collections, edit field values in bulk and export items as CSV.
- [dotsy Content API](https://apis.io/apis/dotsy-ai/dotsy-ai-content-api/) — Read and write page bodies and other content files in a site's draft.
- [dotsy Domains API](https://apis.io/apis/dotsy-ai/dotsy-ai-domains-api/) — Connect custom domains to a site and check their status.
- [dotsy Draft & publish API](https://apis.io/apis/dotsy-ai/dotsy-ai-draft-publish-api/) — Review what changed in the draft, then publish it or discard it.
- [dotsy Forms API](https://apis.io/apis/dotsy-ai/dotsy-ai-forms-api/) — Read the submissions a site's forms have collected.
- [dotsy Guest API](https://apis.io/apis/dotsy-ai/dotsy-ai-guest-api/) — Start a site without an account using a guest token, then claim it later.
- [dotsy Import API](https://apis.io/apis/dotsy-ai/dotsy-ai-import-api/) — Bring an existing website into dotsy from its URL.
- [dotsy Jobs API](https://apis.io/apis/dotsy-ai/dotsy-ai-jobs-api/) — Poll long-running work (imports, bulk content writes, renames) by job id.
- [dotsy Members API](https://apis.io/apis/dotsy-ai/dotsy-ai-members-api/) — Invite people to a site and manage their roles.
- [dotsy Pages API](https://apis.io/apis/dotsy-ai/dotsy-ai-pages-api/) — List a site's pages with their routes, titles and draft state.
- [dotsy Routes API](https://apis.io/apis/dotsy-ai/dotsy-ai-routes-api/) — Map URL paths to content, including redirects.
- [dotsy Sites API](https://apis.io/apis/dotsy-ai/dotsy-ai-sites-api/) — Create, list, read, update and delete sites.
- [dotsy Support API](https://apis.io/apis/dotsy-ai/dotsy-ai-support-api/) — Bug reports and the support inbox. This is rolling out.

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for dotsy: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
